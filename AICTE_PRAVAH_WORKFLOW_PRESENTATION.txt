--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: 30-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Namaste Judges! Har saal hazaron technical institutions AICTE approval (EoA / New Institution)
ke liye apply karte hain. Lekin manual scrutiny, delayed physical inspections, ghost faculties,
aur expiring NOCs ki wajah se approval process slow, opaque, aur vulnerable ho jata hai.

Is problem ko solve karne ke liye humne develop kiya hai 'AICTE PRAVAH' — ek intelligent,
geofenced, end-to-end statutory approval aur compliance monitoring engine, jo AICTE ke
official Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024-27) ke norms par fully automated hai."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: COMPLETE END-TO-END STEP-BY-STEP LIFECYCLE WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STAGE 1: COLLEGE / INSTITUTION PORTAL (Application Submission & Live Tracking)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Screen: College Role Dashboard (Apex Institute of Technology, Bhopal | AICTE-2026-C-1042)
1. Live Workflow Stepper: College ko apni application ka 5-stage visual progress tracker
   dikhta hai: [Draft -> Submitted -> RO Scrutiny -> EVC Physical Audit -> Council Approval].
2. Statutory Document Uploads:
   - Land Title / Registered Lease Deed (>30 years contiguous).
   - Fire Safety NOC from State/Municipal Directorate.
   - Building Structural Stability Certificate (PWD approved, valid 5 yrs).
   - Complete Faculty Cadre Roster with PAN & Aadhaar details.
3. Submit to Council:
   - College 'Submit Application' par click karta hai.
   - Application lock ho jaati hai, status 'Under Scrutiny' ban jata hai, aur file
     automatically Regional Office (RO) ke scrutiny desk par route ho jaati hai.


STAGE 2: REGIONAL OFFICER (RO) SCRUTINY DESK (Desk Audit & Anti-Fraud Radar)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Screen: RO Role Dashboard (Central Regional Office, Bhopal)
1. Scrutiny Queue: Regional applications pending queue me aati hain, sorted by SLA days left.
2. Clause Verification: RO officer documents ko APH 2024-27 norms ke according verify karta hai:
   - Land Norms: Urban (min 4.0 Acres), Rural (min 7.5 Acres).
   - Fire & Stability: Validity check.
3. Automated Ghost Faculty Radar (Anti-Fraud Engine):
   - System automatically har faculty ke PAN number ko pan-India cross-institution database
     se cross-check karta hai.
   - Agar koi faculty doosre college me bhi active roster pe milti hai, to system use instantly
     'Fraud / Concurrent Tenancy' flag karta hai under APH Clause 4.2.b.
   - RO ek click me 'Flag Deficiency' mark kar sakta hai, jisse college ko instant notification
     jata hai rectify karne ke liye.
4. Recommendation:
   - Scrutiny pass hone par RO click karta hai 'Recommend for EVC Physical Inspection'.
   - File automatically next stage (EVC) ko transfer ho jaati hai.


STAGE 3: EXPERT VISIT COMMITTEE (EVC) DESK (On-Site Physical Campus Audit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Screen: EVC Committee Chair Dashboard (NIT Nominee Panel)
1. Blind Allocation: Inspection committee members randomly allocate hote hain taaki koi
   conflict of interest na ho.
2. 200-Meter Anti-Spoof Geofenced Verification:
   - Committee member physically campus visit karta hai.
   - Portal browser GPS geolocation fetch karke college ke registered coordinates se match
     karta hai.
   - Inspection report tabhi unlock hoti hai jab inspector registered campus ke 200m radius
     ke andar physically present ho.
3. Geo-Tagged Campus Evidence Upload:
   - Inspector labs, computer centers (1:4 ratio), workshop, aur Divyangjan barrier-free ramps
     ki live geo-tagged timestamped photographs capture karke upload karta hai.
4. Report Submission:
   - Digital checklist verify karke EVC Chair 'Submit EVC Audit Report' click karta hai.
   - Application directly Central AICTE HQ ko submit ho jaati hai.


STAGE 4: CENTRAL AICTE HQ (Apex Governance & Final Letter of Approval)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Screen: Central AICTE HQ Console (Member Secretary & Chairman Desk, New Delhi)
1. National Governance Overview: Pan-India macro metrics: Total Applications, Approvals,
   Deficiencies, aur SLA breaches across all regions.
2. SLA Escalation Matrix:
   - Agar kisi regional desk par application statutory SLA limit (e.g. 7 days) se zyada delay
     hoti hai, to system automated time-warp alert trigger karke use 'Level-2 Central HQ Desk'
     par escalate kar deta hai. (Demo button: 'Simulate SLA Breach +10d').
3. Final Approval Decision:
   - Council EVC report aur RO recommendations check karke 'Approve Application' par click karti hai.
4. One-Click Digital Approval Docket:
   - System instantly official, digitally signed, QR-Coded AICTE Letter of Approval (LoA)
     generate karta hai with intake breakdown, verified norms, and encrypted QR validation.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3: CORE BACKGROUND ENGINES & COMPLIANCE DIFFERENTIATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Automated Validity Watchdog (Radar Tab):
   - 365-day proactive radar jo Fire NOC, Structural Certificate, aur Affiliations ki expiry
     track karta hai. 60 din pehle automated alerts dispatch karta hai taaki compliance
     lapse na ho.

2. AICTE Pravah Sahayak (AI Statutory Copilot):
   - Bottom-right corner me live floating chatbot powered by Google Gemini AI.
   - APH 2024-27 handbook ke rules par trained: Land norms, 1:20 cadre ratio, EVC guidelines,
     Hindi/English dono me real-time conversational answers deta hai.

3. Immutable Audit Trail (Audit Logs Tab):
   - Har single interaction, status change, document upload, aur SLA breach timestamp, user ID,
     aur IP ke saath permanently log hota hai for 100% transparency.
